How to repeat these checks

Every app got the same checks, in this order, on the Free plan (SortPulse during the Standard plan's free trial). Anyone with a site on that plan can repeat them. How we run every comparison

  1. S1

    Existing pages end up A to Z

    The pages under the space home are sorted with the app, then read back in their stored order. Any consistent A to Z order counts here: how numbers, capitals and accents are ordered is judged in S3 and S4. If the pages come in order only once the page of S2 is added, the result is partial.

  2. S2

    A page added later is put in place

    One more page is created after the sort, and after a short wait its place among the others is read, judged as in S1.

  3. S3

    Numbers sort naturally

    Two pages named like Page 2 and Page 10 must come in number order, not letter order, once the app has sorted them.

  4. S4

    Upper and lower case and accents sort together

    Titles that start with capitals, small letters and accented letters must be mixed in one alphabetical order, once the app has sorted them.

  5. S5

    A nested level is sorted too

    The pages one level down under one parent are read back in their stored order after the sort, judged as in S1. If they come in order only once one more page is added there, the result is partial.
