Questions

What makers ask us

Do you need my permission to test my app?

We read your app's terms first. If they allow publishing test results, we test it like every other app. If they do not, or they are unclear, we ask you first and publish only after a yes. Without a yes, we list the app as not tested and say why.

Can paying change my result?

No. A private report holds the same results as the public page. A re-test is published whatever it shows. A priority test moves the date, not the result.

What if you get something wrong?

You see your results before anyone else and have three working days to correct a fact or object. A correction we can confirm changes the result, and an objection is shown next to it. After publishing, we correct a mistake within three working days and add a dated note to the page.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. The notice, your results before publishing, your right of reply and the badge are free. An app is included because it claims to answer the request, whether its maker pays or not.

Who sees a private report?

Only your team. It holds the same results as the public page, with all the evidence and notes behind them.
