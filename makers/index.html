What is never for sale

  • A better result, a higher place or kinder wording. Rank, verdict and wording are never for sale.

Get your free badge

Every tested app gets a badge with its real count, whatever the result. Nothing to buy, nothing to sign.

  1. Open your app's comparison page and find your app under What each app did.
  2. Follow the Badge link. That image is your badge.

The badge always shows the latest published result. After a re-test it updates on its own.
