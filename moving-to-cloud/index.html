Data Center ends on 28 March 2029

Will your apps come with you to Cloud?

Atlassian ends its Data Center products on 28 March 2029. Its migration assistant tells you whether an app has a Cloud version, not whether that version does the same things. We check that for you.

Send us your list of apps

Tell us the Data Center apps you use, up to 10. We send you a PDF that says, for each one, what you get in Cloud.

  1. Write to us with the names of your apps, as they show in the Atlassian Marketplace or in your app manager.
